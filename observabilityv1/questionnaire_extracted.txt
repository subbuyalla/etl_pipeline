VITHI CONSULTING ASSESSMENT DIAGNOSTIC FRAMEWORK
Observability Maturity Assessment Questionnaire
Customer Discovery, Business Trigger Diagnostic, and Evidence-Based Scoring Across Service, Data Pipeline & AI Estates
CONSULTATIVE ASSESSMENT METHODOLOGYPURPOSE & ASSESSMENT DISCIPLINE: This questionnaire establishes an evidence-backed baseline across telemetry breadth, distributed tracing, alerting precision, SLO governance, data pipelines, and AI data flows. All numerical ratings (Level 1 to 5) must be corroborated by physical evidence artifacts (EV01–EV12). Unverified claims are tagged as 'Unknown' to maintain consulting credibility.

Part 3: Assessment Execution & Governance Flow
The completion of this questionnaire initiates Vithi's structured 4-step diagnostic flow:
Step 1 — Discovery & Trigger Alignment (Day 1): Assessor and client leadership align on business triggers (BT-01 to BT-06) and confirm in-scope assets.
Step 2 — Questionnaire Self-Scoring (Days 2–4): Engineering and data squad leads complete self-scores and provide evidence pointers.
Step 3 — Two-Week Evidence Sprint (Weeks 1–2): Vithi Principal Architects inspect 12 concrete evidence artifacts (EV01–EV12) under read-only access to validate scores.
Step 4 — Executive Readout & Roadmap (Week 3): Formal readout presenting the audited Maturity Scorecard, 1-page executive summary, tool rationalization savings, and 3-horizon transformation roadmap.
